# Head of AI - Northwind Analytics (EJEMPLO FICTICIO)

> Oferta inventada para ilustrar el método. Ni la empresa ni el puesto son reales.

**Company**: Northwind Analytics
**Role**: Head of AI
**Location**: London (hybrid, 2 days/week) or remote within the UK
**Type**: Full-time

## About the role

We are looking for a Head of AI to take our AI systems from prototype to production and to
define how engineering works with AI. Reporting to the CTO, you will lead a team of 6
engineers and own the company's applied AI strategy.

## Responsibilities

- Take models and agentic systems to production, with clear acceptance criteria.
- Define guardrails and evaluation for non-deterministic outputs.
- Control inference cost and the unit economics of AI features.
- Introduce AI-assisted engineering practices across the SDLC.
- Grow and mentor the AI team.

## Requirements

- Proven experience taking AI to production (not just POCs).
- Experience leading engineering teams.
- Familiarity with multi-agent systems and orchestration.
- Professional English.

## Nice to have

- Experience in healthtech or marketplaces at scale.
- Track record as a CTO or founder.
