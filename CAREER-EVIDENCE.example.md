## 0. How to use this file

Every piece of evidence has an identifier (`EV-nn`). A tailored CV is built by
selecting evidence by identifier, not by writing from scratch. Tailoring means
**choosing, ordering and rephrasing** existing evidence, never creating
new facts.

Verification level:

- **V** (verified): it is in the current CV or the candidate has explicitly confirmed it.
- **C** (confirm): it is real but has never been written in these words, or the metric is not settled. It needs a question before it is used.
- **X** (forbidden): a claim that can NOT be made. See section 7.

Strength:

- **A**: hard metric, business figure, attributable result. Works as a headline.
- **B**: solid result, but about process or qualitative.
- **C**: supporting context. It fills, it does not sell.

## 6. Known gaps and how to handle them

They are never filled by inventing. They are acknowledged and offset with what is adjacent.

| Gap | Reality | Usable adjacency | What can NOT be said |
|---|---|---|---|
| GxP, 21 CFR Part 11, system validation | No experience | EV-50, EV-51: audit and traceability culture | That she has worked in GxP or validated environments |
| Teams of more than 16 people | Verified maximum: 16 | EV-40 (doubled it) | Organization sizes that are not in section 2 |

**Offset rule**: a hard gap is never mentioned in the CV. An answer is prepared for the
interview and recorded in the fit report. Every hard gap also has its row in section 10,
so the validator catches it if it slips in.

## 7. Rules of truth

Absolute prohibitions. They apply to the CV, letter, message and profile.

1. **Nothing outside this bank.** If a piece of evidence has no ID, it is not written.
2. **Metrics are not rounded up or recombined.**
3. **Evidence is not moved from one role to another.** Every `EV` is anchored to its `R`.
4. **Evidence marked C is confirmed before it is used.**
5. **No technology is listed in skills without evidence behind it.**
6. **No mention of availability or urgency.** The tone is one of selectiveness.
7. **No long dashes (em-dash) in any text.**
8. **One English variant (UK or US) per document.** If a document goes in Spanish, Spain Spanish.
9. **No invented dates, titles or company names.** Section 2 is the only valid timeline.
10. **Every CV bullet must be defensible for 20 minutes in an interview.**

## 10. Validator watchlist

Terms that `tools/validate-cv.py` looks for in the CV and the messages. It is the only thing
about your career the validator needs to know, and it lives here, not in the code.

- **Term**: literal text (several, comma-separated) or a regular expression between
  backticks. An all-caps literal (`MCP`, `A2A`) is case-sensitive.
- **Level**: `gap` (hard gap from section 6: blocks), `no evidence` (no `EV` behind it:
  blocks), `confirm` (evidence marked `C`: warns).

| Term | Level | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| GxP, 21 CFR, Part 11, computerized system validation | gap | No experience in GxP or validated environments (section 6). |
| MCP, Model Context Protocol | confirm | EV-21 marked C. Confirm what was actually built before sending. |
| A2A | no evidence | Never used it: it cannot be claimed. |
